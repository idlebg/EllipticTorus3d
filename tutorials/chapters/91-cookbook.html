  • See every point of a small curve: All curve points (C): Example 12.1 · Every point of y² = x³ + 7 over F₂₂₃.

Operations

  • Add two points and see the chord: Example 24.1 · G + 8G = 9G: the chord winds three times.
  • Double a point: 2·P₁: Example 25.1 · Doubling (59, 32) on F₉₇: the tangent.
  • Subtract: P₁ − P₂: Example 26.1 · 7G − 2G = 5G on F₂₂₃.
  • Walk G, 2G, 3G, …: P₂ = G and N: Example 27.1 · Step: 5G, then N, gives 6G + G = 7G.
  • Walk the doublings: 2·P₁ and N: Example 27.3 · The doubling walk: G, 2G, 4G, 8G, 16G, 11G.
  • See the GLV relation λ·P = (βx, y): Example 35.1 · G, λG and λ²G share a y.
