# 92 Exercises

Exercises for every level, in the order of the chapters. Each can be done in the visualizer, by hand, or
both. The answers, with a short explanation, are on [93 Answers to the exercises](93-answers.md); every answer there was
computed by the tutorials’ scripts, and those the visualizer can show were checked in it.

## Level 1 · Explorer

1. On F₂₂₃ (preset “Programming Bitcoin”), where is 5G? Give its coordinates and the u, v on its card. (See [12](12-cloud-of-dots.md).)

2. On F₂₂₃, are (47, 71) and (47, 152) mirror images? (See [15](15-mirror-images.md).)

3. How far apart are the public keys 1G and 2G on secp256k1? (See [14](14-how-far-apart.md).)

4. On the sphere, is −G in the northern or the southern half? (See [15](15-mirror-images.md).)

## Level 2 · Learner

5. Check by hand that (116, 55) lies on y² = x³ + 7 over F₂₂₃. (See [21](21-clock-arithmetic.md).)

6. How many points does y² = x³ + 2x + 2 over F₁₇ have? (See [22](22-counting-points.md).)

7. Compute G + 2G on F₂₂₃ by hand. (See [24](24-chord.md).)

8. Find the three points with y = 0 on y² = x³ + 2x + 3 over F₉₇. (See [23](23-negation.md).)

9. On F₁₇, what is (6, 3) − (16, 4)? (See [26](26-subtraction.md).)

10. On F₂₂₃, which k does the mirror image of 5G have? (See [15](15-mirror-images.md).)

11. Walking the doublings of G on F₂₂₃ (operation 2·P₁ and Step), after how many steps are you back at G? (See [27](27-walking-multiples.md).)

## Level 3 · Bitcoiner

12. Which prefix does the public key of private key 9 get? (See [34](34-parity-taproot.md).)

13. Typing G’s x alone (32 bytes), do you get G or −G? (See [34](34-parity-taproot.md).)

14. Are (n−1)/2·G and (n+1)/2·G mirror images? (See [32](32-private-to-public.md).)

15. On secp256k1, which b′ does the point (0, 1) lie on? (See [36](36-invalid-points.md).)

16. What is λG + λ²G on secp256k1? (See [35](35-glv.md).)

## Level 4 · Researcher

17. What is the group structure of y² = x³ + 7 over F₁₀₀₀₉? (See [42](42-group-structure.md).)

18. How many points does the quadratic twist of y² = x³ + 2x + 3 over F₉₇ have? (See [44](44-twists.md).)

19. How many points with y = 0 does y² = x³ + 7 over F₂₂₇ have? (See [43](43-j-zero.md).)

20. A key k satisfies k ≡ 3 (mod 13) and k ≡ 5 (mod 19). What is k mod 247? (See [45](45-invalid-curve-attacks.md).)

21. Is the node y² = x³ − 3x + 2 over F₁₁ split or non-split, and how many nonsingular points does it have? (See [46](46-singular-composite.md).)
