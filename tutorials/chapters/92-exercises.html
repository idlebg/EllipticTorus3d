92 Exercises

Exercises for every level, in the order of the chapters. Each can be done in the visualizer, by hand, or both. The answers, with a short explanation, are on 93 Answers to the exercises; every answer there was computed by the tutorials’ scripts, and those the visualizer can show were checked in it.

Level 1 · Explorer

  1. On F₂₂₃ (preset “Programming Bitcoin”), where is 5G? Give its coordinates and the u, v on its card. (See 12.)

  2. On F₂₂₃, are (47, 71) and (47, 152) mirror images? (See 15.)

  3. How far apart are the public keys 1G and 2G on secp256k1? (See 14.)

  4. On the sphere, is −G in the northern or the southern half? (See 15.)

Level 2 · Learner

  1. Check by hand that (116, 55) lies on y² = x³ + 7 over F₂₂₃. (See 21.)

  2. How many points does y² = x³ + 2x + 2 over F₁₇ have? (See 22.)

  3. Compute G + 2G on F₂₂₃ by hand. (See 24.)

  4. Find the three points with y = 0 on y² = x³ + 2x + 3 over F₉₇. (See 23.)

  5. On F₁₇, what is (6, 3) − (16, 4)? (See 26.)

  6. On F₂₂₃, which k does the mirror image of 5G have? (See 15.)

  7. Walking the doublings of G on F₂₂₃ (operation 2·P₁ and Step), after how many steps are you back at G? (See 27.)

Level 3 · Bitcoiner

  1. Which prefix does the public key of private key 9 get? (See 34.)

  2. Typing G’s x alone (32 bytes), do you get G or −G? (See 34.)

  3. Are (n−1)/2·G and (n+1)/2·G mirror images? (See 32.)

  4. On secp256k1, which b′ does the point (0, 1) lie on? (See 36.)

  5. What is λG + λ²G on secp256k1? (See 35.)

Level 4 · Researcher

  1. What is the group structure of y² = x³ + 7 over F₁₀₀₀₉? (See 42.)

  2. How many points does the quadratic twist of y² = x³ + 2x + 3 over F₉₇ have? (See 44.)

  3. How many points with y = 0 does y² = x³ + 7 over F₂₂₇ have? (See 43.)

  4. A key k satisfies k ≡ 3 (mod 13) and k ≡ 5 (mod 19). What is k mod 247? (See 45.)

  5. Is the node y² = x³ − 3x + 2 over F₁₁ split or non-split, and how many nonsingular points does it have? (See 46.)
